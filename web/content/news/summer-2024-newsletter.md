We're thrilled to announce the official start of our new Special Educational Needs & Disability Unit (SEND Unit) and Safe Motherhood Unit (SMU). While we faced some delays, the project is finally underway.

Our pre-schools are flourishing, with on average 730 children attending each day. Thanks to a generous benefactor, all 12 Early Childhood Development (ECD) centres were able to maintain their feeding programs throughout 2023/24.
