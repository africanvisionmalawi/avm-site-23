**Celebrating 20 Years!**

In 2005, we embarked on our journey to create a healthy, educated, and self-sufficient community in Malawi. This newsletter marks the 20th anniversary of our charity's founding.

Now, in 2025, we're launching the '2025 Pedal Power 500 km Cycle Challenge' through Malawi, alongside other exciting fundraising events.

Our Safe Motherhood Unit (SMU) and SEND (Special Educational Needs & Disability) unit are nearing completion. You can read about these projects and our updated programmes in the newsletter below.
