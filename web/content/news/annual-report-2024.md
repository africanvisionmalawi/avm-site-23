In the coming year, African Vision Malawi is entering an exciting new chapter as we transition our core youth and education work to gold-youth. This partnership will help us reach even more young people, using a proven mentoring model to help them become leaders and find path-ways to employment. While this area of our work evolves, our essential healthcare services will continue to support the community through our trusted local partners.

We are proud to have officially opened the Special Educational Needs and Disability (SEND) unit and the new Safe Motherhood facility, which provides vital care for mums and babies.
