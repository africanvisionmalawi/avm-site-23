## About this event

Form a team of 6 or come and join a team at Long Ditton Village Hall. It will be a fun night with a bar (cash/cards) and lovely homemade curry to spice up your evening. The proceeds help towards our programmes in Malawi to support and empower orphans, ultra-poor adults and people with disabilities. Some fun games in between each round of the Quiz and a good old raffle!
