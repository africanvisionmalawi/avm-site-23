Mark your calendars for Saturday, June 29th, and join us for a fun-filled Indulgence Day, raising much-needed funds for orphans and vulnerable individuals in Malawi.

The event takes place from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM at 31 Upper Brighton Road and offers a variety of activities for everyone. Relax and unwind with donated therapies from generous professionals (schedule on the link below). Browse unique items at market stalls, treat yourself to a delicious home-cooked lunch, delectable cakes, and refreshing beverages. Enjoy the sounds of live music, find your inner zen with a guided yoga session, and enter the raffle for a chance to win fantastic prizes.
