Our amazing 2024 sponsored bike ride through Malawi in now underway. Would you like to join us next time? After incredible cycle rides in 2018, 2019, 2022 and 2024, get inspired by watching one of our [previous videos here!!](https://vimeo.com/352330947)

Please support us as we cycle 500km through Malawi.  Our cyclists  are paying all of their costs to participate; your donation (blue button above) will help them raise funds for the charity’s work in Malawi. Every penny goes to help the most marginalised in society in one of the world’s poorest countries. 

They have 6 cycle days and 2 off-days, one on safari (lions, cheetah, elephant, buffalo, zebra, hippos, birds) the other by the beautiful Lake Malawi where you can feed the Fish Eagles. The trip inscludes a visit to cave paintings and cycle down an incredible escarpment. Don't miss out on the next one!

£1,650 per person is all inclusive for the cycle ride (accommodation, food, transport, cycle hire, safari) but doesn’t include flights, insurance, hike, vaccinations and visa. We ask you to raise at least £1,000 which will go towards our projects. Here are the details of the trip:

If you are interested, please contact [heather.palmer@africanvision.org.uk](mailto:heather.palmer@africanvision.org.uk) for further information.
