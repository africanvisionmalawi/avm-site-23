On Saturday 16th March we are holding a Music for Malawi evening at the cornerHOUSE. 

You can book tickets [here ](https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/whats-on/surbiton/the-cornerhouse-community-arts-centre/music-for-malawi/e-gdxpjr)and see the line-up in the PDF which follows.

[Music for Malawi](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/hh4wbbfo/production/cd73a527756c15186688936562673678025b6afc.pdf)

Music for Malawi is a celebration of music that brings the local community together to help raise money for a safe motherhood unit as well as a special needs therapy unit. This will provide training in nutrition, permaculture gardens as well as giving those with special needs desperate access to therapy and assessments. There is more information about these important programmes within the Health section of our website.
