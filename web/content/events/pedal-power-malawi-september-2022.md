You may have heard about our amazing sponsored bike rides through Malawi over the last few years. Would you like to join us next year? After incredible cycle rides in 2018 and 2019, we have another planned. **THIS WILL TAKE PLACE 18th September - 26th September 2022 for main cycle ride** and if you can, stay on to get involved with projects in our area and be present at the **official opening of Sam's Village on 29th.**** **** **Get inspired by watching the[ 2019 Video here!!](https://vimeo.com/352330947)

Please join in or support us as we cycle 500km through Malawi. We would love to have you join us, but if you can’t, please support our cyclists who are paying all of their costs to participate; your donation (blue button above) will help them raise funds for the charity’s work in Malawi. Every penny goes to help the most marginalised in society in one of the world’s poorest countries. There are  6 cycle days and amongst them, 2 off-days, one on safari, the other by the incredible Lake Malawi. During the cycle ride there is an optional cycle up Zomba Plateau (10km).

£1,750 per person is all inclusive for the cycle ride (accommodation, food, transport, cycle hire, safari) but doesn’t include flights, insurance, vaccinations and visa. We ask you to raise £1,000 which will go towards our projects. If you would like to book or have more information about the trip, contact Heather [here …](mailto: Heather.palmer@africanvision.org.uk)
