**Challenge Yourself for a Great Cause!**

**Climb Mount Snowdon** 
On 6th September 2025 join around 50 of us as we walk, cycle and climb Snowdon. Everything you need to know is in the PDF pack below.

You can do either a walk, bike ride or summit... or all 3 if you are feeling adventurous!

Standing 1,085m above sea level, Snowdon (Yr Wydffa in Welsh) is a popular destination for tourists and recreational ramblers alike. But for the hillwalker, this impressive peak has a treat in store! 

African Vision Malawi are assembling a team, ideally at least 20 for our anniversary; we’d love you to be a part of it.

📅 **Date:** September 6th, 2025
🎉 **Why:** Celebrate AVM's **20th Anniversary** by joining at least 20 amazing participants in this incredible fundraiser.

We’re proud to partner with **Love Support Unite (LSU)** and **The Malawi Projects** to make a difference together.

Don’t miss this unforgettable adventure!
