We are doing a talk at St Matthew's Church, Surbiton, KT6 6JQ on Sunday 7th January starting at 3:30pm about next year's 6-day cycle ride through Malawi. Refreshments, film, presentation and Q&A session. If you are interested, please come along or contact [heather.palmer@africanvision.org.uk](mailto:heather.palmer@africanvision.org.uk) for further information.

Please join in or support us as we cycle 500km.  There are 6 cycle days and 2 off-days, one on safari (lions, cheetah, elephant, buffalo, zebra, hippos, birds) the other by the beautiful Lake Malawi where you can feed the Fish Eagles. You will visit cave paintings and cycle down an incredible escarpment.
