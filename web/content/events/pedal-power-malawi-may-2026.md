**Join Us for the Adventure of a Lifetime in Malawi!**

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Malawi, known as The Warm Heart of Africa, on an unforgettable cycling journey. Pedal through stunning landscapes, visit ancient cave paintings, and experience the impact of the projects your contributions will support. We will help you set up a fundraising page to raise £1,000 to support our sustainable programmes and make a lasting difference.

Highlights of the trip include:

- Cycling down an incredible 20km escarpment

- Going on safari to see wild animals

- Relaxing by the beautiful Lake Malawi

- Visiting Sam’s Village, our eco-build vocational training village

**Trip Details:**

- Duration: 10 days (6 cycling days, 1 safari, 1 day off by Lake Malawi, and 2 days visiting our programmes)

- Cost: £2150 per person (excluding flights and insurance)

- Embark on this adventure and make a difference. Join us in Malawi!
