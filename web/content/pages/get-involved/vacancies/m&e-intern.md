African Vision Malawi, established in 2005, is a charity organization dedicated to improving the lives of children and the vulnerable people in Malawi in the areas of health, agriculture, education, environment and self-sufficiency. We are seeking an analytical M&E Intern to help in monitoring and evaluating the progress of our programmes, ensuring that programmes activities align with strategic objectives leading to realization of their intended outputs in a cost-effective and timely manner.

**Classification**: Full-time.

**Duration**: 3 months.

**Location:** Lilongwe.

**Duties & Responsibilities**

- Assist in designing and implementing monitoring and evaluation frameworks.

- Data collection, cleaning, validation and analyse.

- Assist in preparing reports and presentations on findings.

- Assist in monitoring programme activities and providing feedback on the consistency or discrepancy between planned and actual activities.

- Support programme managers in updating annual activity plan.

- Assist in reviewing performance indicators.

**Required Skills and Qualifications**

1. University degree in Statistics, economics or related Social sciences.

1. More than 3 months’ experience in monitoring and evaluation in NGO or government institution.

1. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

1. Knowledge in statistical software packages i.e. Excel, R, ODK, STATA & SPSS.

1. Ability to work independently and in a team.

**Language requirements**

Fluency in oral and written English and Chichewa

**Benefits**

1. Gain hands on experience in M&E practices within a non-profit organization setting.

1. Mentorship and professional development opportunities.

1. Flexible working hours.

**How to Apply:**

This internship is offered to Malawian nationals only and is offered on a 3 - month basis as a voluntary position. A small stipend is negotiable. Transport and lunch are covered. Please send your resume and cover letter explaining your motivation to join us as intern and how this aligns with our organisational values to: [constance.kainga@africanvision.org.uk](mailto:constance.kainga@africanvision.org.uk) with the subject line 

**“M&E Intern Application” by Friday, 22nd November, 2024.**
